# About me 
Welcome to my website! I am job market candidate for 2026Fall- at McGill University. 
My research interests lie at the intersection of macroeconomics and labor economics, with a focus on how worker heterogeneity shapes firm dynamics and, through them, aggregate productivity.
I am especially interested in the workforce composition firms choose over their life cycle: which workers firms hire as they age and grow, how those choices accumulate inside the firm and shape its productivity, and how the resulting allocation of workers across firms determines aggregate productivity. This makes the price of high-quality workers a central question for young firms in particular, since they rely on such workers to build the capabilities that sustain their later growth.
