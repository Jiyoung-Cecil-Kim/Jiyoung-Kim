Welcome to my website! 

I am job market candidate for 2026 Fall- at McGill University. 

My research interests lie at the intersection of macroeconomics and labor economics, with a focus on how worker heterogeneity shapes firm dynamics and, through them, aggregate dynamics.

I am especially interested in the workforce composition firms choose over their life cycle: which type of workers firms hire as they age and grow and how the choice for workforce quality shapes firm dynamics and its implication on aggregate productivity.
